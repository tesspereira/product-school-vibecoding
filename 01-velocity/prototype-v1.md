## The build

- **What I built:** A catalog that allows FakeCompany employees to find and understand what tools are available and approved for use.
- **Tool used:** Replit
- **Shareable link:** https://fake-company-tool-finder--tesspereira.replit.app

## Show & Swap read

_What a partner understood from your build with no verbal setup, their reaction is your first piece of product evidence._

- **What they understood immediately:** He understands the purpose straight away: a productivity marketplace. Clear "Get hte work done. Start with an outcome" headline at the top of the page. He likes he concept of the search bar and search for a task you want to get done--that works well because it's easy for someone who is not well versed in the tooling. Easy to digest for someone; allows them to input in layman's terms. It's approachable. He expects people of varying expertise so he suggested to expand what's available to include like AI skills (e.g. Atlassian MCP creating Confluence pages, create PRDs, create user stories)--take those skills and give them to a product manager who can use the skills before handing off to a developer/engineer.
- **What confused them:** Not clear what tools are not approved for use. If this is a marketplace, how might I handle an unapproved tool that has all the right skills/capabilities? is there a path to move from unapproved to approved?
- **The assumption they thought you were testing:** He understood what it was testing.
- **The gap between what you intended and what they read:** My partner seemed to understand the prototype. Some additional clarity is needed around the "Unapproved" tools--why show them, what's the path (if there is any) to a conditional approval or limited approval or something in the circumstance where an unapproved tool has useful capabilities.
