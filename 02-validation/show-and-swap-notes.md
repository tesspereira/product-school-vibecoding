# Show & Swap Notes

> Module 2 · Validation. Put the prototype in front of a peer. Their reaction is the data.

## What I showed

https://fake-company-tool-catalog.replit.app

This prototype was meant to test whether the app is intuitive and the info provided was sufficient to make a decision.

## What my partner understood (in their words)

The 5-min. from question to action target was clear. He understood that users struggle to approved tools.

## Where they got confused / pushed back

The kill switch was not clearly described. I built the kill switch but it's not defined on the page.  Additionally, if a tool is not available at all, then what does it mean? versus if it's not available but NOT approved, can I still go it? What is the guardrail for that experience?

## What I'll change as a result

I'll make the kill switch more evident. I would also make the "unapproved" experience/flow clearer.
